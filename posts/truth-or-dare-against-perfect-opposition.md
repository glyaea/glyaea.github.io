name: Truth or Dare Against Perfect Opposition
---

In the game of Truth or Dare, players are made to choose between two options:

1. **Truth.** Answering a particular question.
2. **Dare.** Performing a particular action.

Against perfect opposition, which option should one choose to minimise harm to
themselves? Here is a minimax argument for Truth.

---

A Dare is a request, and a Truth is a request for information. All requests for
information are requests, but not all requests are requests for information.
Thus, all Truths are Dares, but not all Dares are Truths. Let $T$ be the space
of Truths, and $D$ be the space of Dares. Then, we have:

$$
	T\subset D
$$

Thus, choosing Truth strictly reduces the space of issuable requests. A request
can be harmful, and perfect opposition always issues a most harmful request.
Clearly, a most harmful request in $T$ is at most as harmful as a most harmful
request in $D$. Thus, choosing Truth minimises harm.
