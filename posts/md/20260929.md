# A Carnival Puzzle

Suppose a carnival has two games, of which one must be played. Game \(i\) has:

- A four-sided die with up to four good faces.
- A starter score \(S_{i}\in\{0,1,2,3,4\}\).

Playing game \(i\) requires one to roll its die exactly four times and exit
the carnival with score

\[\max(S_{i},\Delta)+S_{3-i}\]

where \(\Delta\) is the number of rolls that landed on good faces. When should one play either game?
